💬 发音建议 | Suggestions
    💡 发音要领 | Pronunciation Tips
    • 一声：高而平，像唱歌的「1」 First tone: High and level, like musical note "Do"
    • 二声：从中音往上扬，像提问的语气 Second tone: Rise from mid to high, like asking a question
    • 三声：先降后升，最低点要到位 Third tone: Dip then rise, reach the lowest point
    • 四声：从高猛降到低，短促有力 Fourth tone: Sharp fall from high to low, abrupt and strong
    • 轻声：轻短模糊，不标声调，时长为前字的1/2 Neutral tone: Short, light, unstressed, half the length of previous syllable
    • 儿化：舌尖上卷至硬腭中部，带卷舌色彩 Erhua: Curl tongue tip to middle of hard palate, add retroflex coloring
